What to ask and prepare before a visit

Use this short checklist when you contact a cleaning service:

  • Name the rooms or surfaces you want cleaned.
  • Separate routine tasks from spots that need extra attention.
  • Mention requests for windows, carpets, floors, or furniture.
  • Ask what type of cleaning is appropriate for your list.
  • Share any questions about sanitizing or disinfecting.

A common mistake is assuming that “cleaning” means the same set of tasks to every customer. Describe the result you want in plain terms and ask what the service includes.
