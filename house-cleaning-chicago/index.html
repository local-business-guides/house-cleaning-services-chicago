A short checklist before you call

Before arranging house cleaning in Chicago, take a few minutes to look around the areas you want cleaned. Write down the tasks you’d like to discuss, then check the list for anything that might be a specialized request. This keeps important details from getting lost when you’re describing the home.

  • Note whether you want ongoing household upkeep or a one-time deep clean.
  • Identify the surfaces that need attention, including floors, carpets, windows, or furniture.
